About
I started at the Academy of Fine Arts in Rome. I ended up working on digital products and services for the public sector across Europe through private organizations. Everything in between was intentional.

My background began in multimedia and technological arts in Rome, originally from Sicily, where I developed an early sensitivity for visual storytelling and digital media. From there, my path moved through photography and later a year in Valencia at UPV exploring directing and motion, before culminating in a Service Design MSc at the University of Bologna — where I learned how to listen to people, understand their needs, and translate those needs into meaningful design decisions.

Today I work at the intersection of product strategy, service design, and stakeholder management — shaping digital products for Smart Cities, B2G environments, and private clients who need both rigour and adaptability.

I'm a highly collaborative and attentive designer, naturally extroverted and creatively driven. I work closely with teams and stakeholders, turning conversations and diverse perspectives into clear, actionable design directions. I design through dialogue - listening carefully, making sense of people’s needs, and building solutions grounded in real contexts rather than assumptions.
