About
I trained as an artist and became a designer of products and services. What connects both is making sense of complexity and giving it a form people can use.

Fine Arts in Rome, a year of directing and motion in Valencia, then an MSc in Service Design in Bologna. Originally from Sicily, I learned to see through art, and to listen through design.

Today I work across research, strategy, UX and product: understanding complex problems, connecting the perspectives of users, stakeholders and technical teams, and turning insight into products and services that hold up in the real world — from 0→1 B2B SaaS to Smart City platforms.

I design through dialogue. I listen closely, make sense of what people need and facilitate the conversations that turn diverse viewpoints into clear priorities, grounded in real contexts rather than assumptions.
